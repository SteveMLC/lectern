This is Lectern — an open-source replacement for Sessionboard's programme side: call for papers, review, speaker operations, and the published agenda, in one Cloudflare-native app. [[slnc 675]]

Every conference gets a public home: the programme, the schedule, and a speaker gallery, all rendered from the same data the organizer manages. [[slnc 2025]]

The call for papers is a real form with optional submitter accounts and conditional logic. Speakers can create an account for a proposal-status dashboard, or submit without one and recover their private link by email. [[slnc 1125]] Pick Workshop as the format and a required workshop-length field appears. The API enforces the same rule, so a hidden field is never demanded. [[slnc 900]] Co-presenters attach with role labels, and they stay attached through review, acceptance, and the published session. [[slnc 1125]] Submitting shows a reference id on screen and sends a receipted confirmation email — every send in Lectern is logged with a receipt. [[slnc 2700]]

The organizer console is one passcode, published deliberately so judges can walk in. [[slnc 2025]]

The new proposal is already in Reviews, with its speakers and their bios. Decisions are a two-step flow. First a committee note — notes stack per reviewer name, so a solo organizer can escalate to a committee without accounts. [[slnc 900]] Then Lectern drafts the acceptance email from that note, for human review before anything sends. [[slnc 1350]] Deliver and approve: the decision, the note, and the receipted email land together, and the speaker's portal updates instantly. [[slnc 1575]]

Accepted talks become sessions. Auto-place fills the unscheduled ones against rooms and conflicts. [[slnc 900]] Publishing is an explicit action with a receipt — and schedule notices carry each speaker's exact slot, never fired by a drag. [[slnc 2250]]

The public schedule updates immediately: time, room, track, and format on every block, with calendar files and embeddable widgets behind it. [[slnc 2475]]

Every speaker has a personal portal behind a capability link: their proposals with live status and editing, their tasks with due dates, uploads, and their own itinerary. [[slnc 2475]]

Organizer-side, every deliverable is versioned and exportable as a ZIP, and every email ever sent sits in the outbox with its receipt. [[slnc 2025]]

Lectern. Open source, MIT — and measured against the organizer's own eval kit. The score, the method, and every verdict are in the repo.
